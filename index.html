About Us

A family flower shop on Bridge Street

Blooming Bridge Florist is a small, family-owned shop in the heart of Newcastle West. I'm Liga, and flowers have been my trade for more than 25 years — the last 8 of them here in this shop, getting to know the town and the people in it.

We keep things simple: fresh flowers, honest prices, and arrangements made by hand for the occasion you're celebrating — or the one you're grieving. Whether it's a last-minute birthday bunch or a full wedding order, we treat every arrangement the same way we'd want one made for our own family.

— Liga, Blooming Bridge Florist
